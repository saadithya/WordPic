About WordPic

WordPic transforms your images into stunning text-based art (ASCII art). It’s perfect for artists, developers, and anyone who loves creative visualizations.

Features

  • Convert any image (PNG, JPG, WebP) into ASCII or text art
  • Adjust width, contrast, characters, and background
  • Export as .txt, .html, or .png
  • Offline processing – images never leave your device
  • Optional premium upgrade for unlimited PNG downloads

Privacy & Security

WordPic processes all images locally within your browser. No files are uploaded or stored externally. Premium users may verify licenses via Gumroad’s secure API (HTTPS only).
